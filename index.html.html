Dragonfly Vision

Your camera, seen through a dragonfly's eyes, split for a Google Cardboard viewer.

  1. Tap Start and allow camera and motion access.
  2. Tap Cardboard view, turn the phone sideways and slot it into the viewer.
  3. Flip the switches on your box, or use the controls panel before you put it on.

Camera and motion sensors only work on a secure (https) page.
